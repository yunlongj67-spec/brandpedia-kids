## How to play

1. Double-click `BrandPedia-Pitch-Video.html` (or open it in Chrome/Edge).
2. Click **▶ Play video** (browsers require this click before audio/voice can start).
3. It auto-advances scene-by-scene; subtitles show at the bottom and the AI voice reads them.

**Controls**
- **Space** — play / pause
- **M** — mute / unmute the AI voiceover
- **F** — fullscreen
- **← / →** — previous / next scene
- Click any chapter bar (top) to jump · **↺ Replay** to restart

## The AI story this video tells

The brief asks for *"how AI plays a role in the full procedure and how to reach the target with AI assistant."* The video answers it in two movements:

1. **AI as the engine (Scene 5):** the full generation pipeline — synthesize → LLM script → voice → image → assemble → store. This shows AI doing the work at every stage of producing a lesson.
2. **AI as the assistant (Scene 6):** the kid-facing loop — ask → find/make → teach → remember. This shows the AI *guide* helping each child reach the learning goal, and the data flywheel that makes it improve.

Scenes 7–8 then ground it: the same architecture lets AI scale from a zero-cost demo to production, and the generated-content model is what makes the business economics work.

## Export to MP4 (turn the HTML into a video file)

Any of these works; **QuickTime is the fastest on a Mac**:

### Option A — QuickTime screen recording (easiest, native)
1. Open `BrandPedia-Pitch-Video.html` in Chrome, press **F** for fullscreen, then **▶ Play**.
2. Open **QuickTime Player → File → New Screen Recording** (or `Cmd+Shift+5`).
3. Record the full screen for ~4:58, then **Stop**. Save as `BrandPedia-Pitch.mp4`.
> Tip: mute notifications (Focus mode) so they don't appear in the recording.

### Option B — ffmpeg screen capture (command line)
```bash
# list audio/screen devices first
ffmpeg -f avfoundation -list_devices true -i ""
# capture screen index :1 (adjust) for ~4:58 (298s)
ffmpeg -f avfoundation -framerate 30 -i "1:" -t 298 BrandPedia-Pitch.mp4
```

### Option C — headless automation (no UI)
Use Puppeteer/Playwright to drive the page and pipe frames to ffmpeg. Slower to set up but fully scripted and repeatable; reach out if you want this pre-wired.

### Optional polish
- For a **human voiceover**, mute the AI voice (press **M**), play the file, and read the narration column above into your mic — the on-screen timing is calibrated to a natural speaking pace (~140–150 wpm).
- The subtitles are already on-screen, so the exported video is accessible without extra work.

## Notes & troubleshooting

- **No voice?** Click **▶ Play video** first (browsers block speech until a user gesture). Then check **Voice: ON**. Chrome/Edge have the most reliable English voices.
- **Voice over/under-runs a scene?** Voice speed varies by OS/browser; the on-screen timeline is fixed. If needed, pause to let a line finish, or mute and read live.
- **Want it shorter/longer?** Scene durations live in the `SCENES` array at the bottom of `BrandPedia-Pitch-Video.html` — edit the `dur` values (seconds) and the timeline + chapter bars recompute automatically. Current total: **298s (4:58)**.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Product-led growth first — then schools, then brands.

🚀

1. Product-Led Growth (now)

Free "explore any brand" is the viral hook. Kids share brands they generated; parents convert to paid.

👨‍👩‍👧

2. Communities & creators

Parent forums, education influencers, and kid-focused creators drive low-CAC B2C signups.

🏫

3. B2B school pilots

Course pack + teacher dashboard. Land 3–5 pilot schools, then expand district-wide.

🤝

4. Brand partnerships

Once traction is visible, onboard brands for official deep-dive pages and campaigns.

Solo-founder friendly: PLG front-loads organic growth before any enterprise sales motion.

Here’s an English version of the speech framework. It keeps the same 4.5‑minute structure, with clear timing, visual cues, and content points. You can fill in your own project details.

---

🎯 Round 1 Pitch Video – Speech Framework (4 min 30 sec)

---

Part 1: The Hook (0:00 – 0:30)

· Visual: Full‑screen shot of you (talking to camera)
· Content:
  · Introduce yourself (name)
  · One sharp sentence about the pain point (a specific, relatable frustration)
  · Present your project name and one‑line value proposition

---

Part 2: Problem Amplification (0:30 – 1:15)

· Visual: Screenshot / chart / diagram showing how bad the current situation is
· Content:
  · Why existing (non‑AI) solutions fail
  · Use data or a story to make the judge feel the pain (e.g., “It currently takes X hours…”)

---

Part 3: Core Demo – Show Your Product! (1:15 – 3:30) ★CRITICAL★

· Visual: Full‑screen browser recording of your live website (with you in a small picture‑in‑picture window, or just voiceover)
· Content – walk through your key features one by one:
  1. Brand Search – type a query, show the result, explain that content is AI‑generated
  2. AI Voice Narration – click the speaker icon, play a short audio clip
  3. AI Q&A – type a question, show the real‑time answer
  4. Flashcards / Knowledge Cards – show how AI automatically extracts key points

---

Part 4: Behind the AI (3:30 – 4:00)

· Visual: Switch to your Cursor IDE, show your core code / prompt template (no need to run it, just scroll)
· Content:
  · Which model you use (e.g., GPT‑4o‑mini, DeepSeek, etc.)
  · How you design your system prompt – especially for safety (e.g., “always reply in child‑friendly language, no violence or ads”)

---

Part 5: Before & After + Close (4:00 – 4:30)

· Visual: Back to full‑screen shot of you
· Content:
  · Quantify the transformation: “Before it took 2 hours; now it takes 3 seconds.”
  · One strong sentence on why your AI makes the old way obsolete
  · Thank the judges and give a confident sign‑off

---

💡 Tips for Using This Framework

· Practice each part so your total time stays between 4:00 – 4:30.
· For the demo (Part 3), prepare a fixed test case (e.g., search “Disney”) and rehearse it until it works smoothly.
· You don’t need to actually connect the API – just show the UI and explain the AI logic. The framework above is fully compliant with the official requirements.
